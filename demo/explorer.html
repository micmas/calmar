About this demonstration

These images and outputs have not had a human quality check. The reports include approved, in-review and draft knowledge-base findings, with their status shown in each report. These are research examples, not diagnoses.

03 / Follow the workflow

How the pieces fit together

Read the complete notebook ↗
  1. 01

    Prepare the images

    Prepare the scans and align them with a reference brain so locations can be compared.

  2. 02

    Map the lesion

    Compare automated lesion outlines with expert outlines, then locate the affected brain regions.

  3. 03

    Estimate disconnection

    Use reference white matter pathways to estimate which connections the lesion may disrupt.

  4. 04

    Connect to evidence

    Bring the imaging measurements together with published findings, their sources and review status.

People, software and AI

People set the research questions, choose the methods and assess the results. AI assists with coding and evidence extraction. It does not replace image quality checks, source verification or clinical judgement. The code and saved notebook make the work available for inspection and reuse.
