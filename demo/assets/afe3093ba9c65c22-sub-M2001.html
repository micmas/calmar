Preprocessing was performed with QSIPrep 26.1.1 (Cieslak et al. 2021), which is based on Nipype 1.11.0 [Gorgolewski et al. (2011); Esteban et al. (2025); RRID:SCR_002502] and follows the NiPreps framework (Esteban et al. 2019).

Anatomical data preprocessing

The T1-weighted (T1w) image was used as the anatomical reference throughout preprocessing. It was corrected for intensity nonuniformity with N4BiasFieldCorrection (ANTs 2.6.0) (Tustison et al. 2010). The anatomical reference was reoriented into AC-PC alignment with the rigid (6-degree-of-freedom) component of an affine registration to the MNI152NLin2009cAsym template (Fonov et al. 2009). A nonlinear registration from AC-PC space to the template was then estimated with symmetric normalization (SyN) in antsRegistration (Avants et al. 2008). ROI masks of abnormal tissue were incorporated into the registration. The brain was extracted from the T1w image with SynthStrip (Hoopes et al. 2022), and the image was segmented with SynthSeg (Billot, Greve, et al. 2023; Billot, Magdamo, et al. 2023), both from FreeSurfer 8.2.0.

Diffusion data preprocessing

DWI data were denoised with Marchenko-Pastur principal component analysis (MP-PCA) (Veraart, Novikov, et al. 2016; Veraart, Fieremans, et al. 2016) as implemented in dwidenoise from MRtrix3 3.0.4 (Tournier et al. 2019), with a window of 5 voxels (chosen automatically). Volumes with b < 100 s/mm² were treated as b=0 images.

Head motion was corrected with DIFFPREP from TORTOISE V4.2.0 (Irfanoglu et al. 2025) in its “quadratic” mode, which corrects rigid head motion together with linear and quadratic eddy-current distortions. DIFFPREP registered each volume to the first b=0 volume. The transform had 14 estimated parameters: 6 for rigid-body motion and 8 for linear and quadratic eddy-current distortion along the phase-encoding axis. The b-matrix was rotated with the estimated motion.

No susceptibility distortion correction was performed.

The b=0 reference was coregistered to the anatomical reference with a rigid transform estimated by antsRegistration (ANTs 2.6.0). The registration was initialized by a global search over rotations with antsAI, so that large differences in head orientation between the DWI and anatomical scans were recovered.

Framewise displacement (FD) was calculated from the head-motion estimates with the implementation in Nipype (following the definitions by Power et al. 2014). FD, the head-motion estimates and the slicewise quality measures from head motion correction were saved as per-volume confounds.

The DWI series were resampled into AC-PC space at 2 mm isotropic resolution with windowed-sinc (Lanczos) interpolation, or linear interpolation where the output voxels were more than 10% smaller than the input voxels. The transforms not already applied were combined so that this was a single resampling step. Any gradient nonlinearity, susceptibility distortion or (with DIFFPREP) eddy-current correction applied in this step was Jacobian-modulated (after DRBUDDI, TORTOISE’s intensity-correction maps were applied instead); head motion and coregistration were not modulated. The b-vectors were reoriented with the affine part of each volume’s head motion and coregistration transforms. After resampling, B1 field inhomogeneity was corrected with dwibiascorrect from MRtrix3 3.0.4 (Tournier et al. 2019) using the N4 algorithm (Tustison et al. 2010).

Many internal operations of QSIPrep use Nilearn 0.10.1 (Abraham et al. 2014, RRID:SCR_001362) and DIPY 1.12.1 (Garyfallidis et al. 2014). For more details of the pipeline, see the processing pipeline details in QSIPrep’s documentation.
